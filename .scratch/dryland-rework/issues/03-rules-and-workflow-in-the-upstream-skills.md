# Rules and workflow in the upstream skills

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

Inventory the explicit and implicit rules, states, artifacts and hand-offs of the unmodified upstream Matt Pocock skills (wayfinder, grilling, triage, to-spec, to-tickets, implement, implement-spec, code-review, pr, retro and the rest of the main flow), as the baseline for extracting a deterministic workflow. Mark which rules are mechanical and checkable and which need judgement. Source: the installed skills mirror in this repo and upstream.

## Answer

Rules, states, artifacts and hand-offs of the 12 main-flow skills are inventoried and each rule is marked mechanical, judgement or hybrid in .scratch/dryland-rework/research/upstream-skill-rules.md. Findings: every skill has a checkable shell (ordering, enums, sections, gates) around a judgement core; six human-gate events and the claim/resolve/index sequence are what an engine must own. Upstream was not re-fetched; the installed copy was trusted from the earlier byte-identity check.
